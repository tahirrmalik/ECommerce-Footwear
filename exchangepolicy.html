EXCHANGE & RETURNS

We want you to love every pair you receive from Footwear Luxe. If something isn't right, we're here to make it easy. Our 7-day exchange and return policy is designed to give you complete confidence when shopping with us.

Return & Exchange Window

You may request an exchange or return within 7 days of receiving your order. Requests made after this window will not be accepted, so please inspect your order as soon as it arrives.

The 7-day window begins from the date of delivery as recorded by our courier partner, not from the order placement date.

Conditions for Return / Exchange

To be eligible for a return or exchange, the following conditions must be met:

  • The item must be unused, unworn, and in its original condition.
  • The item must be returned in its original packaging, including the box, tags, and any accessories.
  • The item must not show any signs of wear, damage, or alteration caused by the customer.
  • Proof of purchase (order number or receipt) must be provided.
  • Sale or discounted items are only eligible for exchange, not refund.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstances:

  • Items that have been worn, washed, or altered.
  • Items without original tags and packaging.
  • Items purchased during final clearance sales.
  • Items damaged due to misuse or negligence by the customer.
  • Custom-made or personalized orders.

How to Request a Return or Exchange

Follow these simple steps to initiate your return or exchange:

1

Contact Us

Email support@yourfootwear.com or WhatsApp us within 7 days of delivery with your order number.

2

Get Approval

Our team will review your request and send you a Return Authorization along with instructions.

3

Ship the Item

Pack the item securely in its original box and send it to the address provided by our team.

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will process your exchange or refund within 3–5 business days.

Exchange Process

Exchanges are subject to stock availability. If your requested size or color is not available, we will offer you a store credit or refund. Exchanges are processed within 3–5 business days of receiving the returned item.

You will be responsible for the return shipping cost unless the exchange is due to a defect or error on our part, in which case we will cover the shipping fees.

Refunds

Approved refunds will be issued via the original payment method. Please allow 5–7 business days for the refund to reflect in your account after approval.

  • Cash on Delivery orders: Refunds will be issued via bank transfer. Please provide your bank account details at the time of requesting a refund.
  • Online payments: Refunds will be returned to the original payment source.
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

Defective or Wrong Items

If you receive a defective, damaged, or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery. Send clear photos of the issue to support@yourfootwear.com and we will arrange a free replacement or full refund immediately.

In case of any defect or wrong delivery, all return shipping costs will be fully covered by Footwear Luxe. You will not bear any additional expense.
