SHIPPING POLICY

At Footwear Luxe, we are committed to delivering your order swiftly and safely. Please read our shipping policy carefully to understand how we process and ship orders across Pakistan. For any questions, our support team is available 24/7.

Delivery Coverage

We currently deliver to all major cities and towns across Pakistan, including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, and thousands of smaller localities via our courier partners.

International shipping is not available at this time. We hope to expand beyond Pakistan in the near future.

Processing & Delivery Times

All orders are processed within 1–2 business days (Monday–Saturday, excluding public holidays) after payment confirmation.

Free Shipping

Enjoy free shipping on all orders with a cart value of Rs 14,999 or above. The discount is automatically applied at checkout — no coupon code required.

Free shipping applies to the subtotal before any other discounts. It is valid on all delivery zones within Pakistan.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive an SMS and/or email containing your tracking number and the name of our courier partner. You can use this to track your shipment in real time on the courier's website.

If you do not receive tracking details within 3 business days of placing your order, please contact us at support@yourfootwear.com or call +92 300 1234567.

Packaging

Every pair of shoes is packaged in our signature Footwear Luxe box and wrapped securely to prevent damage during transit. We take great care to ensure your order arrives in perfect condition.

Delays & Issues

While we do our best to meet all delivery timelines, delays may occasionally occur due to high demand seasons, adverse weather conditions, or courier service disruptions.

If your order is significantly delayed or you suspect it has been lost in transit, please reach out to us immediately and we will investigate and resolve the issue promptly.
