PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters deeply to us at Footwear Luxe. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are committed to protecting your data and being transparent about our practices.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use our website or place an order:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Order Information: Products purchased, payment method, and order history.
  • Account Information: Username and encrypted password if you register an account.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on the site, browser type, and device information.
  • Communication Data: Messages and emails you send to our support team.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Process and fulfil your orders and arrange delivery.
  • Send order confirmations, shipping updates, and customer support responses.
  • Improve our website, products, and overall shopping experience.
  • Send promotional emails and offers (only if you have opted in).
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions and maintain security.
  • Comply with legal obligations where required.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Courier Partners: Your name, phone number, and delivery address are shared with our logistics partners solely for the purpose of delivering your order.
  • Payment Processors: Payment details are handled securely by trusted payment gateways. We do not store full card details on our servers.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your data if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request.

We will never sell your personal data to advertisers or data brokers. Your information is used only to serve you better.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them to remember your preferences, keep items in your cart, and understand how visitors use our site.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect the functionality of certain parts of the website such as the shopping cart.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the unsubscribe link.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@yourfootwear.com.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our website uses HTTPS encryption for all data transmission.

While we strive to protect your data, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong password and keep your account credentials confidential.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed.
