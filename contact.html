FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within Pakistan. Express delivery (1-2 days) is available for an additional fee.
Can I return or exchange my order?
Yes! We offer a 7-day return/exchange policy. Items must be unused, in original packaging, and accompanied by the receipt.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery?
Yes, Cash on Delivery (COD) is available across all major cities in Pakistan. Online payment via Visa/Mastercard is also accepted.
How do I track my order?
Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via SMS and email. You can use this to track your delivery in real-time.
Are your sizes true to fit?
Our shoes are true to international sizing (EU). We recommend referring to our size guide on each product page. If you're between sizes, go up.
